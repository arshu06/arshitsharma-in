AboutNot a chef, not a photographer, not a writer

Based near the Mata Chintpurni Mandir, separated by almost two hundred kilometres from everything I like. My entire horizon is swallowed by the Dhauladhar mountains, and I spend my time pointing a simple phone camera at them.

What I do all day

I work in operations. In practice that means I spend my day looking at what went wrong yesterday, working out whether it will go wrong again tomorrow, and building something small so that it does not.

Most of that something small is spreadsheets and scripts. A dashboard nobody asked for that turns out to be the thing everyone opens first. A report that used to take an afternoon and now takes a keystroke. It is unglamorous work and I am better at it than at anything else I have tried.

It is also the reason this site looks like this. I wrote every page of it by hand. There is no newsletter box, no cookie banner, no tracker counting how far down you scrolled. If something here is broken, I broke it.

What is on here

Three things, and they are not related except by being mine.

Weekly notes. What the week actually had in it. I fill in a sheet most days, so these get written from something I wrote down at the time rather than from what I can still remember on a Friday. Some weeks have a lot in them. Some are a Monday repeated seven times. Both go up.

Photographs. Mostly one mountain range, at an unreasonable number of different hours. Almost all of them taken within a few kilometres of my house, on a phone, with no editing worth mentioning.

Food reviews. I am in Tricity often, seeing friends I have had for years, and mostly we eat and drink. I score every restaurant properly and publish all the working, so you can disagree with the number rather than just with me. How I rate explains the whole thing, including why I am in a position to say a place was bad.

Things worth knowing

I like almost everything. All kinds of music, all kinds of food, all kinds of drink. My friends once sent back a pasta at Pizza Hut that I had no problem with whatsoever, and that happens more often than it should.

Most of my days are not interesting. I wake up later than I meant to, log into my shift, work, doomscroll, watch something, sleep. That is genuinely most of it, and it is part of the reason the weekly notes exist. A week where nothing happened is still a week, and writing it down is the only way I ever notice the pattern in it.

I read in binges rather than steadily. Seventy chapters of the same web novel in a single day is a normal amount for me.

I did not start paying proper attention to anything until I began writing reviews on Google Maps and had to explain myself. Having to justify an opinion in public turns out to be the fastest way of finding out whether you had one. That is more or less the whole site.

I started it because one meal was good enough that I wanted to say so somewhere that would last. Appreciating something properly is harder than complaining about it, and that is still the reason this exists.
