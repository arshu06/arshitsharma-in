I live near Mata Chintpurni Mandir. The Dhauladhar takes up the whole horizon from my balcony and I have photographed the same range at more hours than is reasonable.

I work in operations, which mostly means reports and small tools that make other people's jobs less tedious. Some of that will end up here.

I am in Tricity a lot, seeing friends I have had for years, and mostly we eat and drink. I score every restaurant I go to and publish the working. I like almost everything, which turns out to be useful, because when I tell you a meal was not worth the money it took some doing.
