I like almost everything I eat. My friends once sent back a pasta I was perfectly happy with, and that is fairly typical of me. Which means when I tell you a meal was not worth the money, it took some doing.

The best thing I have ever eaten is a plate of peri peri fries at a place in Chintpurni, near where I live. Two hundred rupees, and it is the dip that does it. I have since paid ₹878 a head in Connaught Place and given that place 16 out of 30.

I started this because one meal was good enough that I wanted to say so somewhere permanent. That is still the point, even when a review says the opposite.
