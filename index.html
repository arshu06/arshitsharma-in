I live in the Kangra valley, where the Dhauladhar sits across the whole horizon. I have taken an unreasonable number of photographs of it doing more or less the same thing at different hours, and some of them are here.

Twice a month I come down to Chandigarh and Mohali to see friends, and mostly we eat. I score every restaurant I go to and publish the working. I like almost everything, which turns out to be useful, because when I tell you a meal was not worth the money it took some doing.

The best thing I have ever eaten is a plate of peri peri fries at a place in Chintpurni. Two hundred rupees. I have since paid ₹878 a head in Connaught Place and given that place 16 out of 30.

Weekly notes are coming. Whatever else I end up building will land here too.
