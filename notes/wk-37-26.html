7 to 13 September 2026The week I did not write down

PUBLISHED 14 SEPTEMBER 2026

I did not fill anything in this week.

I kept thinking I would do it on Sunday. Sunday went past. Then this morning I did not have time before my shift, and now it is Monday evening and I am sitting here trying to remember what happened between Monday and Friday, and most of it is simply gone.

This is what I get. My About page says I fill in a sheet most days and that it is the only reason I can tell you what I did on a Tuesday three weeks ago. This week I did not, and I cannot.

So here is what survived.

The tournament

Monday was a lot of work. Tuesday was work and some PUBG with Simar. I could not tell you a single other thing about either day.

By Wednesday the PUBG had turned into a tournament, which is how these things go. Nobody declares one. You just play a third match and somebody starts keeping score.

I went 2-1 up.

Now. My About page says I win every game I play. I am going to be honest about this one.

Thursday I went to the bank with my grandma, and when I got back Simar beat me badly. Not narrowly. Badly. 2-2.

We agreed on a decider for Friday.

Friday

Friday was unexpectedly busy at work, the kind of busy where you look up and the day is somehow already over.

I do not usually ask anyone on my team to stay past the shift. That day Simar and I agreed we were playing the decider after log-off, and we sat there for an hour after the shift officially ended.

Before we started, Simar told me he was going to order food in after he won and eat it in peace.

He did not order anything that day.

I won.

That satisfaction. I have nothing more intelligent to say about it than that.

The outfit

Saturday I went to Hoshiarpur to try on the wedding outfit. Something was off with one of the pieces, not the kurta, so it had to be sorted and I went back on Sunday.

Then I came home and showed the clothes to Hadesh, and that fucker told me nobody wears a kurta to a day wedding, and that the three-piece suit would be overdressed for the cocktail.

I love that kurta and I love that suit. I asked him before I placed the order, and he had a different opinion then.

The timing was not in his favour either, because I happened to be making his presentation at the time. So the presentation became a hostage, and then I said a number of things to him that are not going in here even on this website.

Then I rang Gagan for a second opinion. Gagan said it looks good.

Anyway. As some wise man said, you can never be overeducated or overdressed.

The blip

That is the whole week. Five days of work, a PUBG tournament, two trips to Hoshiarpur and an argument about clothes I had already paid for.

There might have been more. I genuinely do not know, and that is the point. I have spent six weeks writing that you only see the pattern if you write it down, and then I skipped a week and the week went with it.

Writing it down daily is not the discipline part. Remembering to is.

Anyway. Aman's wedding starts this week. I am in Chandigarh from Friday and I am not back for a fortnight, which means the next two notes are going to arrive together and they are going to be long.

I am filling the sheet in every day for those. Obviously.
