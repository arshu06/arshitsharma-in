Oven Fresh, HLP GalleriaA better Chili's than Chili's, by a single point

PUBLISHED 2 AUGUST 2026

The Reorder

3 in 4 I would go back.

Four dishes. Three I would order again.

Total23 / 30 Food8 / 10 Value8 / 10 Service4 / 5 Room3 / 5 Per head₹650

There is a Chili's in this mall. I ate there too, and I will get to it. The short version is that Oven Fresh is pretending to be an Indian Chili's, and it is doing a better job of being Chili's than Chili's is, for a difference of one rupee a head.

That is not a joke about the price. That is the actual arithmetic. ₹650 here, ₹649 there. And when I sat down and scored them both properly, Oven Fresh came out 23 and Chili's came out 22. One rupee apart, one point apart.

The part that still makes me laugh: the best thing I ate at Oven Fresh was a chicken enchilada. A bakery café in a Mohali mall, whose sign says bakery and café, beat an American chain at its own food, upstairs from where that chain was serving it.

We went at a peak hour without booking and waited for a table, which tells you something before you have eaten anything. The place was full. Somebody greeted us inside a minute and walked us over.

What to order

  • OrderChicken enchilada
  • OrderRoasted garlic bread, and ask for a dip
  • OrderBBQ chicken alfredo
  • SkipAchari paneer tikka pizza

Only the paneer pizza misses. Three from four, and the one I would put money on is the enchilada.

The room

It looks like its photographs, which is rarer than it should be.

Clean, well lit enough that you can photograph food without turning the flash on, comfortable an hour in, and the table was big enough to hold everything we ordered without anyone playing Tetris. Nobody had to raise their voice. The music sat where music should sit.

It is a room that works rather than a room that performs. For ₹650 a head that is the correct trade.

I would bring a date here. I would bring my parents here. I would not sit here on my own for an hour, but almost nowhere in a mall passes that test.

The food

Chicken enchilada. I did not order this one. A waiter suggested it and it turned out to be the best thing on the table. The proportions are right, which sounds like faint praise and is not. Most versions of this are a fight between the chicken, the sauce and the cheese, with one of them winning. This one was balanced.

BBQ chicken alfredo. The reason I came. It delivered, arrived hot, and was worth what it cost. No complaints, and no story either.

Roasted garlic bread. Good, and it needs a dip. Not because there is anything wrong with it, but because plain garlic bread gets boring faster than the portion size allows. Give people something to put it in.

Achari paneer tikka pizza. Too achari for me. Everyone else at the table was fine with it. This is the one thing I would not order again, and the one thing that was not worth its price.

Something on the menu was unavailable that night.

The portions

This is my actual complaint, and it is a strange one to have.

The quantity is too much. Most of these dishes are not built for one person to finish, and we did not finish. They warned us about portion sizes when we ordered, to their credit, and we ordered anyway.

There was a point in the meal where I knew I could not eat any more and could not make myself stop. That is a good problem, but it is still a problem, and it means you should order fewer dishes than feels right.

The service

The waiters here are the reason this is not just a decent meal.

One person looked after us throughout. Somebody went out of their way for us. Somebody said something to us on the way out, which almost nobody does. The whole floor felt like people who were glad you had come in.

I will be honest about the gaps, because a review that says everything was perfect is not worth reading. Nobody checked in after the food landed, and at one point I had to flag somebody down rather than catch an eye. On a full night at peak hour, that is forgivable. It is also not nothing.

The bill

₹650 a head and the bill felt right. No surprises on the menu, no charges I had not counted on, no service charge quietly added.

This is the first place I would pay exactly the same money for exactly the same meal again, without hesitating.

The verdict

I have eaten at another Oven Fresh. This one was better, and the brand is clearly being held to a standard here rather than coasting on the name.

I thought about this meal the next day. Everyone at the table said they wanted to come back. I left with a stomach full of good food and an evening with my friends that I actually enjoyed, which is the thing restaurants are supposed to be for and the thing most of them forget.

Go with friends. Go with family. Go on a date if you want to eat properly rather than perform. Order less than you think you need.
